Environment

The environment is everything around us that supports life and influences how living organisms survive and develop. It includes both natural and human-made components such as air, water, soil, plants, animals, forests, rivers, oceans, buildings, and cities. A healthy environment is essential for clean air, safe drinking water, food production, and human health. However, pollution, deforestation, climate change, population growth, and excessive use of natural resources are putting increasing pressure on the environment. Therefore, protecting the environment is the responsibility of everyone. We can contribute by reducing pollution, saving water and electricity, planting trees, using resources wisely, reducing waste, and following sustainable practices. A clean and balanced environment is necessary for a healthy life and a better future for coming generations.
